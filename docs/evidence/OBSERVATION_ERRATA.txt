OBSERVATION LIMITS IDENTIFIED DURING REVIEW — 31 OCTOBER 2026

The frozen contract and oracle files are retained byte-for-byte. Two statements
need narrower interpretation in the evidence delivered by this implementation:

1. ORACLE_INTERPRETATION.txt promised individual worker timestamps. The trace
   contains original worker messages, PIDs, barriers/events, exit codes and the
   SQLite audit sequence, but no per-worker timestamps. No timestamp measurement
   or exact wall-clock overlap/commit instant is claimed. The deterministic
   registry clock serves token validity tests, not performance measurement.

2. C25/C26 use two live child processes but explicitly release the second call
   only after the first returns. They exercise cancel-first and commit-first
   serialization orders, not measured cancel/commit lock contention. C20/C23/C37
   do release two admission processes from one barrier against shared SQLite.
   No finite set of these runs proves every interleaving or distributed atomicity.

These observations do not change any expected verdict, effect or accounting
oracle. They limit what can be inferred from the preserved traces.
